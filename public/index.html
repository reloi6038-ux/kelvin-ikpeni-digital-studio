Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you use?

I specialize in the Node.js stack — meaning I use JavaScript/TypeScript on both the frontend and backend. My core tools are Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and vanilla CSS/JS, though I am always adapting to the best tool for the job.

Do you build custom designs or use templates?

Everything is built from scratch. I do not use WordPress themes or generic templates. Every line of code and every pixel is crafted specifically for your brand and requirements.

How long does a project take?

It depends entirely on the scope. A simple portfolio takes 1-2 weeks, while a complex full-stack web application or marketplace can take 4-8 weeks. We will establish a clear timeline before starting.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes! I offer ongoing maintenance and support to ensure your website stays secure, updated, and performs flawlessly as your business grows.
