Privacy Policy

This site collects limited information only to respond to inquiries and to keep the site secure.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we store your name, email address, subject, and message in a secured MongoDB database. We also store a hashed (non-reversible) version of your IP address to prevent spam — we never store your raw IP.

How we use it

Contact form submissions are used only to reply to your message. We do not sell, share, or rent this data to third parties.

Cookies

This site uses one authentication cookie when an administrator logs into the admin dashboard. That cookie is httpOnly and inaccessible to JavaScript. No tracking cookies are used.

Your rights

To request deletion of any message you've sent, contact the contact form with the email address you used.
